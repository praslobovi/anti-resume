A systems-architecture blueprint mapping the physical and cognitive ecology of human neuroplastic healing. Developed for highly sensitive, hyper-divergent neural hardware: this design applies the physics of Dissipative Structures to neuroplastic restructuring using a three-step model—Bifurcation, Re-organization, and Quenching—supported by a multi-disciplinary, composite approach integrating scaffolding of physiological, somatic, environmental, and computational feedback systems to safely guide and lock in conscious, deliberate cognitive restructuring.
